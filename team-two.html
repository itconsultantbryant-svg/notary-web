Meet The Team

Licensed Notaries & Legal Officers

Detailed profiles of our certified notaries and legal officers who serve individuals, businesses, and government partners throughout Liberia.

Hon. Jefferson S. Teah

Head Notary Public · Commissioner of Oaths

Hon. Jefferson S. Teah is the founder and Head Notary Public of the institution. A native of Picnicess Sobobo Town, Grand Kru County, he is a Commissioner of Oaths committed to professionalism, accuracy, and the proper authentication and certification of documents in accordance with Liberian law. He leads the institution's notarial practice and its standards of public service.

Joseph K. Tweh

Lead Notary Public · Commissioner of Oaths

Joseph heads our notarial practice with eighteen years of experience authenticating deeds, affidavits, and corporate instruments. A Supreme Court-appointed Commissioner of Oaths, he oversees quality control and ensures every seal we issue meets Liberian legal standards.

Fatima A. Kromah

Senior Legal Officer

Fatima advises clients on document validity and regulatory compliance before notarization. A graduate of the Louis Arthur Grimes School of Law, she specializes in cross-border contracts and estate documentation for institutions across Monrovia.

Emmanuel B. Saye

Commissioner of Oaths

Emmanuel administers oaths and affirmations for sworn statements and statutory declarations. With a background in court administration, he brings precision and discretion to sensitive affidavit and immigration matters.

Mary G. Davis

Licensed Notary Public · Document Examiner

Mary is a certified document examiner who verifies identities and detects altered records prior to sealing. She manages our high-volume personal-notarization desk and mentors junior staff on signature authentication best practices.

Augustine W. Doe

Legal Officer · Contract & Deed Specialist

Augustine handles property transfers, lease agreements, and commercial deeds requiring witnessed execution. He coordinates on-site mobile notary visits for corporate clients and maintains our secure record-keeping archive.

Patience L. Jackson

Affidavit & Certified Copy Specialist

Patience prepares and notarizes affidavits for immigration, education, and family matters, and produces certified true copies of original documents. Known for clear client guidance, she helps first-time notary clients understand exactly what to bring.
