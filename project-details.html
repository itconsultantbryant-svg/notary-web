Property Transfer Documentation

Deed Authentication & Witnessing

We support property buyers, sellers, and their legal representatives with the notarization, witnessing, and certification of land and property transfer documents in line with Liberian requirements.

Overview

Property transfers involve multiple signed documents — deeds, agreements, and supporting affidavits. Our notaries verify the identity of each party, confirm the documents are complete and voluntarily signed, and apply the official seal so the paperwork is accepted by the relevant authorities.

Our Process

01
Document Review

We check the deed and supporting papers.

02
Identity Verification

All parties present valid photo ID.

03
Witness & Seal

Signatures witnessed and sealed.

04
Certified Handover

Certified documents returned to client.

Outcome

The client receives fully notarized and certified transfer documents, properly executed and ready for submission. As with all our case work, details are kept strictly confidential and handled in accordance with Liberian notarial law.
