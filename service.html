What We Offer

Comprehensive Notarial Services

From a single signature to complex institutional documentation, our licensed notaries cover the full range of notarial needs in Monrovia and beyond.

Notarization and Authentication of Documents

We notarize and authenticate documents by verifying their proper execution and compliance with applicable notarial requirements, helping establish their validity and credibility.

Learn more

Certification of Signatures and Affidavits

We certify signatures and affidavits to confirm that documents have been properly signed or sworn before a Notary Public in accordance with applicable requirements.

Learn more

Acknowledgment and Execution of Legal Documents

We formally acknowledge the execution of legal documents, confirming that the parties signed voluntarily and understood the nature of the documents presented.

Learn more

Notarization of Contracts and Agreements

We provide professional notarization of contracts and agreements to help establish the authenticity of signatures and the proper execution of contractual documents.

Learn more

Affidavits and Sworn Statements

We administer oaths and notarize affidavits and sworn statements for individuals who are required to make formal declarations under oath.

Learn more

Powers of Attorney

We notarize Powers of Attorney authorizing an individual or entity to act on behalf of another person, subject to applicable legal and notarial requirements.

Learn more

Declarations and Legal Instruments

We notarize various declarations and legal instruments, ensuring that documents presented for notarization are properly executed and meet applicable professional standards.

Learn more

Certification of Copies Where Legally Permissible

Where permitted by law, we certify copies of documents by confirming that the presented copy corresponds with the original document or meets the applicable certification requirements.

Learn more

Business and Corporate Documents

We provide notarial services for business and corporate documents, including resolutions, agreements, authorizations, declarations, and other documents requiring notarization.

Learn more

Property-Related Documents

We provide notarial services for eligible property-related documents, including declarations, authorizations, agreements, and other instruments requiring notarization.

Learn more

Letters, Authorizations, and Official Declarations

We notarize eligible letters, authorization documents, and official declarations to provide formal verification of signatures, statements, or execution where required.

Learn more

Documents Intended for Use Within Liberia and Internationally

We assist with the notarization of documents intended for official use in Liberia or abroad, subject to the applicable requirements of the relevant authorities and jurisdictions.

Learn more

Other Notarial Services Permitted Under the Laws and Professional Standards of Liberia

We provide other lawful notarial services within the scope of our authority, guided by applicable Liberian laws, regulations, and professional standards.

Learn more
