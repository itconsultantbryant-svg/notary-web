About Us

Hon. Jefferson S. Teah Notary Office

Welcome to Hon. Jefferson S. Teah Notary Office, a professional notarial services office committed to providing reliable, accurate, confidential, and professional services to individuals, businesses, institutions, and organizations in Liberia and beyond.

Located at Bye Pass, Bassa Community, Techno House, Monrovia, Liberia, our office provides professional notarial services designed to authenticate, certify, and formally execute documents in accordance with applicable laws, regulations, and professional standards of the Republic of Liberia.

At Hon. Jefferson S. Teah Notary Office, we understand that notarization is more than simply affixing a signature or stamp to a document. It is a professional responsibility that requires careful attention to identity, authenticity, proper execution, documentation, and compliance with the law. We therefore approach every document presented to our office with diligence, impartiality, and professionalism.

  • Certified Document Notarization
  • Affidavits & Sworn Statements
  • Contract & Deed Witnessing

Our Mission

Our Mission

The mission of Hon. Jefferson S. Teah Notary Public Office is to provide professional, impartial, reliable notarial services that protect the integrity and authenticity of documents entrusted to our Office.

We are committed to verifying identities, witnessing and authenticating signatures, administering oaths and affirmations, acknowledging the proper execution of documents, and performing other lawful notarial acts with the highest standards of integrity, accuracy, confidentiality, and professionalism.

Our mission is to uphold the trust and confidence of the public by ensuring that every notarial act is performed responsibly, independently, and in accordance with the applicable laws and professional standards of the Republic of Liberia.

Through continuous improvement, responsible use of technology, and client-centered service, we strive to contribute to a credible, transparent, accessible, and modern notarial system in Liberia, while maintaining the independence and impartiality that are fundamental to the notarial profession.

Our History

Our History

The Hon. Jefferson S. Teah Notary Public Office was established following the appointment of Hon. Jefferson S. Teah as a Notary Public by His Excellency George Manneh Weah on September 14, 2023. Since its establishment, the Office has remained committed to integrity, professionalism, confidentiality, and excellence in the delivery of notarial services.

On January 16, 2026, Hon. Jefferson S. Teah was reappointed by His Excellency Joseph Nyuma Boakai, Sr., reaffirming the Office's commitment to serving the Liberian public.

The Office has since grown into a structured institution with six (6) employees, led administratively by the Chief of Office Staff (COS), James B. Kollie, supporting its daily operations and institutional development.

On August 15, 2026, the Office achieved another major milestone with the launch of its official website, embracing digital transformation and expanding access to its services and information.

Today, the Hon. Jefferson S. Teah Notary Public Office continues to pursue a vision of modern, reliable, transparent, and professional notarial services, with a strong commitment to continuous improvement and alignment with international standards.

Our journey is one of trust, growth, innovation, and an unwavering commitment to professional excellence.

Our Leadership

Head Notary Public

Meet the commissioner leading our institution's notarial practice.

Hon. Jefferson Sietiejaleh Teah

Commissioner of Oaths & Head Notary Public — Monrovia, Liberia.

Hon. Jefferson S. Teah is a distinguished Liberian citizen, born on August 15, 1987, in Picnicess Sobobo Town, Grand Kru County, unto the union of Mr. Harris N. Teah and Mrs. Sophia W. Teah. From early childhood he developed a strong sense of responsibility, discipline, and commitment to personal development — values of respect, perseverance, humility, and service that continue to shape his professional journey.

Beginning his primary education in Picnicess Sobobo Town in 1993, he later migrated to Monrovia in 2002 to continue at St. Philip's Ecumenical High School, and in 2008 enrolled at E. Johnathan Goodridge Memorial High School in Barnersville Estate, Montserrado County. Through every stage he demonstrated determination to overcome challenges and remain focused on his goals.

His commitment to public service and belief that citizens have a duty to contribute positively to society led him into Notarial Practice, where he upholds professionalism, accountability, accuracy, and respect for the law. He continues to aspire to become a leading figure in the notarial profession and an example of responsible citizenship.

Educational Background

Hon. Jefferson S. Teah began his primary education in Picnicess Sobobo Town, Grand Kru County, in 1993. In 2002 he migrated to Monrovia, where he continued his education at St. Philip's Ecumenical High School until 2007. Due to a change in residence, in 2008 he enrolled at E. Johnathan Goodridge Memorial High School in Barnersville Estate, Montserrado County, where he continued to pursue his academic aspirations. His movement from Grand Kru County to Monrovia exposed him to new experiences, opportunities, and responsibilities.

Commitment to Public Service

It has always been his desire to contribute meaningfully to the development of Liberia and to serve his fellow citizens. His passion for service, professionalism, integrity, and dedication to duty led him into Notarial Practice, where he upholds the principles of professionalism, accountability, accuracy, and respect for the law. Through his work he has distinguished himself as one who values the proper authentication and certification of documents and understands the important role notarial services play in the legal and administrative processes of the Republic of Liberia.

Vision and Legacy

Hon. Jefferson Sietiejaleh Teah believes that education, integrity, hard work, discipline, and service to humanity are fundamental pillars for personal and national development. His journey — from a rural community to the nation's capital — is a story of determination, resilience, and an unwavering desire to build a meaningful life through service and professional excellence. He remains committed to the ideals of integrity, professionalism, service, and excellence, convinced that the progress of Liberia depends on citizens willing to dedicate themselves to the advancement of their communities and nation.
