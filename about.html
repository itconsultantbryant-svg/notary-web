About Us

Professional, Confidential, and Legally Sound Documentation

Our institution is staffed by licensed and experienced notaries committed to accuracy, confidentiality, and full legal compliance. We serve individuals, businesses, and government partners across Monrovia and beyond, providing the assurance that every document we handle carries the full weight of Liberian law.

Our Mission

Our Mission

To provide accessible, trustworthy, and fully compliant notarial services to every individual, business, and institution in Monrovia and across Liberia. We believe that legal documentation should never be a barrier — it should be a bridge to opportunity, security, and peace of mind.

Through transparent pricing, clear guidance, and meticulous attention to detail, we make the notarization process straightforward and reliable for everyone who walks through our doors or engages us remotely.

Our History

Our History

The Notary Institution was founded to serve the growing documentation needs of Monrovia's residents, entrepreneurs, and institutions. What began as a small practice has grown into a trusted name in Liberian notarial services.

With more than 20 years of combined experience among our licensed notaries, we have certified thousands of documents — from personal affidavits to complex corporate agreements — always upholding the highest standards of integrity and legal compliance.

Credentials
