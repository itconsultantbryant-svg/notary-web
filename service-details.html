Document Notarization

Certifying Your Most Important Documents

Our core service verifies the identity of signers and certifies that signatures on legal, business, and personal documents are genuine, voluntary, and properly executed under Liberian law.

Our Process

01
Submit Documents

Bring or send the document(s) to be notarized.

02
Verify Identity

We check a valid government-issued photo ID.

03
Notarize & Seal

Signature witnessed, seal and record applied.

04
Deliver Certified Copy

Your notarized document is returned to you.

Required Documents Checklist

  • Valid government-issued photo ID (passport, driver's licence, or national ID)
  • The complete, unsigned document(s) to be notarized
  • Any supporting references or exhibits named in the document
  • Witnesses, if your document requires them

Frequently Asked Questions

Do not sign the document in advance — you must sign in the notary's presence. Bring a valid photo ID and the full, completed document.

A current government-issued photo identification such as a passport, driver's licence, or national ID card is required to verify your identity.
